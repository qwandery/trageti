# Paitan Broth Notes (fictional)

In paitan-style broths, opacity is not a flaw. A sustained rolling boil helps disperse fat, gelatin, and tiny protein-rich solids through the liquid. The goal is a stable suspension with body, not a clear stock with a fat slick on top.

Short simmering extracts flavor but not enough body. Low heat can make a broth cloudy in a muddy way without creating the milky texture associated with strong paitan. Time, bones, water ratio, and vigorous agitation all matter.
